Lucy is the channel's virtual host. She explains complicated AI and technology topics in a relaxed voice for viewers who are not engineers.

我们怎么做内容 · How we make our films

每个选题先查官方页面和权威来源，再写脚本；影片中的事实都能对应到来源。我们不制造恐慌，也不夸大。

Every topic starts from official pages and reliable sources before the script is written. Facts in the films map back to those sources. We do not sensationalise.

影片使用AI辅助制作（配音、画面），并在平台上按要求标注“AI生成/合成内容”。

Our films are produced with AI assistance (voice and imagery) and are labelled as AI-generated or synthetic content on each platform where that label is offered.
