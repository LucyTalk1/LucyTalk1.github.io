我们如何使用TikTok · How our app uses TikTok

“Lucy Talk Uploader”是露西Talk频道运营者自用的发布工具，只用来把频道自己制作的原创视频发到频道自己的TikTok账号 @lucytalk1。它不向公众开放，没有其他用户。

"Lucy Talk Uploader" is a tool used by the operator of the Lucy Talk channel only. It posts the channel's own original videos to the channel's own TikTok account, @lucytalk1. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

发布流程 · The posting workflow

  1. 运营者在自己的电脑上完成视频和文案。
    The operator finishes the video and caption on their own computer.
  2. 运营者用TikTok账号登录并授权（Login Kit）。TikTok显示所请求的权限。
    The operator logs in with the channel's TikTok account and authorises the app through Login Kit. TikTok shows the requested permissions.
  3. 应用确认已连接的是 @lucytalk1（只读取头像、昵称）。
    The app confirms the connected account is @lucytalk1 (it reads only the avatar and display name).
  4. 运营者选择一个成品视频，检查文案，点击上传；视频通过 Content Posting API 发送到该账号。
    The operator picks one finished video, checks the caption and clicks upload; the video is sent to that account through the Content Posting API.
  5. 运营者可在TikTok“设置和隐私 → 安全 → 管理应用权限”随时撤销授权。
    The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.

我们访问的数据 · Data we access

权限 Scope用途 Why
user.info.basic确认连接的是频道自己的账号（open ID、昵称、头像）。 Confirms the connected account is the channel's own (open ID, display name, avatar).
video.upload把频道自己的成品视频以草稿形式发到该账号。 Sends the channel's own finished video to that account as a draft.
video.publish经运营者逐条确认后，发布频道自己的视频。 Publishes the channel's own video after the operator approves each post.

我们不读取任何其他TikTok用户的数据，不做广告投放或用户画像，不出售、不共享令牌或数据。访问令牌只保存在运营者的私人电脑上。

We do not read any other TikTok user's data, show ads or profile anyone, and we never sell or share tokens or data. Access tokens are stored only on the operator's private computer.
