隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · 最后更新：2026年10月15日

本政策适用于露西Talk网站和“Lucy Talk Uploader”应用。Lucy Talk Uploader是露西Talk频道运营者自用的发布工具，只把频道自己的视频发到频道自己的TikTok账号 @lucytalk1，不向公众开放。

This policy covers the Lucy Talk website and the "Lucy Talk Uploader" app. The app is a tool used by the operator of the Lucy Talk channel only, to post the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account @lucytalk1. It is not offered to the public.

网站 · This website

本网站是静态网站，没有登录、评论或注册，不设置分析或广告Cookie，不收集访客的个人信息。托管服务商可能按其政策记录标准的访问日志。

This website is static. It has no login, comments or sign-up, sets no analytics or advertising cookies and collects no personal information from visitors. The hosting provider may keep standard access logs under its own policy.

应用访问的TikTok数据 · TikTok data the app accesses

  • Basic account information of the channel's own account (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, to confirm the right account is connected. · 频道自己账号的基本信息，用于确认连接正确。
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload and publish the channel's own videos. · TikTok签发的访问令牌，只用于上传和发布频道自己的视频。

使用与存储 · Use and storage

数据只用于把露西Talk自己的视频发布到已连接的账号。令牌只保存在运营者的私人电脑上，不出售、不共享、不转让给第三方；不用于广告或用户画像；不收集其他TikTok用户的数据。

Data is used only to post Lucy Talk's own videos to the connected account. Tokens are stored only on the operator's private computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Data is not used for advertising or profiling, and no other TikTok user's data is collected.

保留与删除 · Retention and deletion

令牌只在使用期间保存。你可随时在TikTok“设置和隐私 → 安全 → 管理应用权限”撤销授权，撤销后令牌将被丢弃。如需删除数据，请发邮件联系我们。

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions; on revocation stored tokens are discarded. To request deletion, email us.

儿童 · Children

本网站和应用不面向13岁以下儿童。

The website and app are not directed at children under 13.

变更与联系 · Changes and contact
