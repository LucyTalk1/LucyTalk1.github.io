Privacy Policy – Lucy Talk Uploader

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Lucy Talk Uploader ("the app") is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the 露西Talk / Lucy Talk channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account (@lucytalk1). It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok account information (open ID, display name, avatar) of the channel's own account, used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload and publish the channel's own videos.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and publish videos created by Lucy Talk to the connected TikTok account.
  • Tokens are stored locally on the owner's private computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • The app does not collect data from other TikTok users and does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; on revocation, stored tokens are discarded.
